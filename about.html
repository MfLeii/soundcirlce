About Us – Sound Circle

🎯 Vision

To create a world where every student feels seen, heard, and connected—through the music that defines them.

🛠️ Mission

We empower young people to build authentic friendships by transforming personal playlists into safe, meaningful social connections.

🌟 Problem

Many students feel isolated and struggle to connect, especially those too shy to approach others. Music offers comfort and identity, yet there’s no space that turns playlists into opportunities for genuine social interaction.

💡 Solution

Sound Circle connects students through shared playlists, turning music taste into friendship discovery. The app reduces isolation, fosters belonging, and builds authentic social connections through music-driven engagement.

🚀 Go-To-Market Strategy

Launch in Metro Manila universities, then expand to Cebu and Davao. Partner with campus orgs, student influencers, and event organizers to spread awareness.

📊 Key Metrics

  • ✔ Playlist match rate
  • ✔ Daily active users
  • ✔ Retention & engagement time
  • ✔ Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

🔒 Positioning

Sound Circle is not a dating app. It’s friendship-first, built around emotional safety, identity, and belonging — powered by the universal language of music.

✨ Tagline

Playlists become friendships.
